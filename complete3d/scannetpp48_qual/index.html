Complete3D — ScanNet++ val, reconstruction from 32 views: qualitative results

All 48 Murre-paired scenes of Table 1 (official ScanNet++ validation split, 32 input DSLR views). Methods: Complete3D-TS (estimated depth = VGGT points; sensor depth = scan points), GenRecon, DA3 + TSDF, VGGT-Ω + TSDF, LingBot-Map + TSDF, FineRecon, Murre + TSDF. Per scene, 6 of the 32 input frames and 4 of the official held-out test frames are drawn once with a fixed seed and reused for every method.

Section 1 shows each method's full output mesh from one shared camera per scene (40° elevation, whole scene in frame, ceiling and upper walls cut at the median input-camera height for every mesh, the point cloud and the GT laser mesh). Colour where the method produces it; FineRecon and the GT-geometry still are grey. Section 2 renders each mesh at the 4 held-out DSLR poses the 2D protocol scores, next to the real photographs at those frames. Black patches on some TSDF meshes are those methods' own missing vertex colours, rendered as is.

Rendered 2026-10-25 by [eval] (scripts/render_scannetpp48_qual.py, same camera / cut / shading as the Replica page); cameras, seeds and frame ids per scene in scenes/manifest.json and scenes/<scene>/scene.json. Click any image for full size. Companion page: Replica, 32 views.
