Observed = within 15 cm of what the first station scanned; unobserved = laser-GT surface that station never saw. The input has no geometry in the unobserved region; the merged result recovers 0.918 of it (recall at 10 cm).

Merge rule

  • Free space = 2 cm voxels crossed by each laser ray (station → point), stopping 4 cm before the hit.
  • A predicted vertex is dropped if it lies in free space (the scan saw nothing there) or within 3 cm of a scan point (the scan already has the surface); a predicted face survives only if all three vertices do. Merged = scan mesh ∪ surviving predicted faces.
  • Leakage check: 7.9% of the raw predicted surface (mean over scenes) lies in space the scan saw empty and is removed; 72% of predicted faces are kept on average.
