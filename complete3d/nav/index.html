Complete3D — Navigation on the raw ScanNet++ scan vs our reconstruction from 32 frames

Simulation readiness: PointNav in Habitat on two worlds per scene — S = the raw ScanNet++ laser scan, R = our Complete3D-v2 reconstruction of the same scene from 32 DSLR frames plus VGGT-Omega evidence computed from those same frames (no depth sensor, no scan) · 120 ScanNet++ test scenes × 50 point-goal episodes sampled on the floor of S and carried to R (start / goal snapped within 0.25 m; an episode that cannot be placed or connected on R counts as a failure) · the same two frozen agents on both arms (DD-PPO depth, DD-PPO RGB), greedy actions, Gibson PointNav settings (0.25 m / 10°, success within 0.2 m, 500 steps) · SPL is scored against the scan's own shortest path on both arms · image-only benchmark · scan-conditioned · VGGT-evidence ·

Agents on identical episodes (120 scenes, 6,000 episodes)

Δ = R − S with a 95 % paired bootstrap CI over scenes. Unplaceable = episodes whose start or goal is more than 0.25 m from R's navmesh or disconnected on R (counted as failures in SR/SPL). Runnable-only = SR / SPL on R over the placeable episodes alone. The RGB agent sees flat-shaded vertex colours on both arms (no textures), so its S row is a rendering-domain control, not a ceiling; the depth agent is the geometry claim. Geometry of the reconstruction against the laser mesh (137 scenes): Chamfer 0.0505, F-score @10 cm 0.874.

Map level (medians over scenes)

Computed on the navmeshes the agents walked, on one shared 5 cm raster per scene. Phantom free space = navigable on R only; hole-filled = the scan has no floor there (a scan hole, closed morphologically), hallucinated = the scan has floor but no free space; lost = navigable on S only. Path ratio = R's shortest path / S's shortest path per episode (< 1: the scan's holes were filled; > 1: detours).

Top-down maps are cut-away colour renders of each mesh (everything above floor + 1.6 m removed) with the navmesh difference tinted on the R column — red free space lost on R, green phantom free space on R that fills a scan hole, blue hallucinated free space — and the agent's trajectories of six selected episodes (navy success, orange failure; dashed = the navmesh shortest path; ● start, ★ goal; ✕ = the S start of an episode that cannot be placed on R). Videos replay one episode: the agent's own camera on S (left) and on R (middle) with a picture-in-picture of the policy's input (the depth image or the 256² RGB image), and the walked paths on the top-down map (right; navy S, teal R); the replay is deterministic and reproduces the recorded trajectory step for step.
