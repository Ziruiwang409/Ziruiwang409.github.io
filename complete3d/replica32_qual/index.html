Complete3D — Replica, reconstruction from 32 views: qualitative results

All 16 paired scenes of Table 1 (Replica, 32 input views). Methods: Complete3D-TS (estimated depth = VGGT points; sensor depth = scan points), GenRecon, DA3 + TSDF, VGGT-Ω + TSDF, LingBot-Map + TSDF, FineRecon, Murre + TSDF. Per scene, 6 of the 32 input views and 4 held-out views are drawn once with a fixed seed and reused for every method.

Section 1 shows each method's full output mesh from one shared camera per scene (40° elevation, whole room in frame, ceiling and upper walls cut at the median input-camera height for every mesh and the point cloud). Colour where the method produces it; FineRecon and the GT-geometry still are grey. Section 2 renders each mesh at the 4 held-out camera poses the Replica 2D protocol scores, next to the ground-truth renders at the same poses. Black patches on some TSDF meshes are those methods' own missing vertex colours, rendered as is; the GT held-out images are the protocol's habitat renders and are darker than the unlit mesh colours.

Rendered 2026-10-25 by [eval] (scripts/render_replica32_qual.py); cameras, seeds and view ids per scene in scenes/manifest.json and scenes/<scene>/scene.json. Click any image for full size.
